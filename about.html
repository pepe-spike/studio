Pepe Spike explores how content can shape design, and how design, in turn, can shape content. The studio is dedicated to the development of creative projects, from concept and creative research to design.

Pepe’s practice focuses on graphic design for publishing and communication, with an emphasis on printed matter, alongside with visual identities, art direction, web design.
It is guided by a critical awareness of the tools and methodologies of communication and distribution.

Pepe Spike is Noa Merlini, Agnese Pozzobon and Martina Valente.
