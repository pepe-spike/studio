About
Pepe Spike is a studio dedicated to the development of creative projects – from concept development and creative research to design and production – guided by a critical awareness of the tools and methodologies of communication and distribution, jointly run by Noa Merlini, Agnese Pozzobon and Martina Valente.

The studio practice focuses on visual and graphic design for publishing and communication, with a primary emphasis on printed matter, alongside work in data visualization and web design, visual identities, and art direction.

Pepe Spike works across design, fashion, and art, embracing a porous vision that seeks spaces of contamination – exploring how content can shape design, and how design, in turn, can shape content.
